# Bleakonomics

Welcome to Bleakonomics: bringing the most important macro charts to the people, free and open.

The goal is simple: take economic charts that usually live behind jargon, terminals, subscriptions, or scattered government pages, and make them readable, current, and easy to share. Bleakonomics is a public macro dashboard for people who want to understand the economy without needing a Bloomberg terminal or a finance degree.

## Current Charts

### 10 Year - 2 Year Treasury Spread

Tracks the spread between 10-year and 2-year U.S. Treasury yields, one of the most watched recession indicators in macroeconomics. Negative spreads usually mean markets expect stress ahead.

More charts are coming soon.

## Status

Bleakonomics is early. The first chart is live, the architecture is taking shape, and the mission is to keep adding useful charts one at a time without overcomplicating the site.
